# Make Me A Monster!
Talk to NPCs as something other than yourself. Make Me A Monster replaces your character's chat head in dialogue boxes with the head of almost any* NPC in the game, from a goblin to a dragon to the Wise Old Man to the Almighty Saradomin himself, and keeps it there until you change your mind.

\* Heads that are blank, exact duplicates of another, or have parts that don't move with the face aren't listed.

# What it does
## Override your chathead
* Your head becomes theirs. Pick an NPC and its head appears in your place in every dialogue box: The NPC you are talking to keeps their own head.
* Over 5,000 heads to choose from. Almost every NPC in the game with a chat head is listed, grouped by name, race or job. Search by name or id, then pick a variant if the group has several looks
* Browse before you pick. A gallery window shows one head from each group or every head, on paged, resizable grids with a search box, and a variants window shows every head in the chosen NPC's group, with a button to its other groups if it has any. Click any head to use it
* Change the size. A slider scales the head from half size up to three times larger, allowing NPCs with all sizes of heads to be used.
* Keep the settings where you like. They sit on the plugin's page, and the pop-out button in its top right corner moves them to a window of their own and back.
## Override your chathead animation
* Choose the expression. Chat heads animate, and you choose which animation yours plays: happy, angry, scared, drunk, evil laugh and more.
* Leave it on default and your head follows the mood of each line, the way the game intended.
* Make any character do an evil laugh, or make the gods look afraid!
# Requirements
PrettyUI, which the plugin manager installs alongside it.
